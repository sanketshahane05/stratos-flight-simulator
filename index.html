    Gesture guide
    

      
Left stick
Pitch and roll. In Ground view, move the lookout.

      
Swipe up / down
Set throttle on the right side. In Ground view, adjust height.

      
Tap / double-tap
Toggle smoke / change camera on the right side.

      
Hold
Hold the right side for boost; lift your finger to stop.

      
Swipe left / right
Start a left or right roll during level flight.

      
Pinch
Zoom the ground camera with two fingers on the right side.
